The story

Sadie Chase has spent most of her life fighting brain cancer, with her devoted single father, Tom, at her side. Her one escape is Billy, a funny, fearless boy with bone cancer she met in an online support group. The day he finally says "I love you," Sadie overhears that her time is running out, and Billy goes silent. Holding him to their promise, she persuades Tom to drive her from Los Angeles to Denver. Along the way, a roadside mechanic, a kitschy alien shop, and a detour through Tom's Nevada hometown reopen old wounds, including the mother who walked out on them.

A funny, heartbreaking father-daughter road movie about living fully in the time you have.

From the writer-director

"I wrote Road to Recovery in the middle of my grief."

SCOUT TAYLOR-COMPTON, TO DEADLINE

Scout Taylor-Compton wrote the film after losing her mother to cancer, drawing on her own experience of loving someone through the end.

An actress and filmmaker known for playing Laurie Strode in Rob Zombie's Halloween films and Lita Ford in The Runaways, she founded Building Blocks Productions to write, direct, and produce her own work. Road to Recovery is the company's first production.
